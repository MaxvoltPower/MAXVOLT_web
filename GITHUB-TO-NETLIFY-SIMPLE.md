## 📋 Now Do This (One Step At A Time)

### **STEP 1: Go To Your GitHub Repository**
1. Open this link: https://github.com/MaxvoltPower/MAXVOLT
2. You should see your repository

---

### **STEP 2: Upload All Website Files**

**Method 1: Upload via GitHub Web (EASIEST - No Software Needed)**

1. Click the big green button that says **"Code"**
2. Click **"Upload files"**
3. A window opens asking you to drag and drop files
4. You have 2 options:

**Option A: Upload Everything At Once**
- Drag the entire `maxvolt-website` FOLDER into the GitHub upload area
- Wait for upload to finish
- Click **"Commit changes"** (green button at bottom)
- Done!

**Option B: Upload Piece By Piece**

This is like putting toys in a box one by one:

1. First, upload `index.html`
2. Then upload `netlify.toml`
3. Then upload the `css` folder (with styles.css inside)
4. Then upload the `js` folder (with main.js inside)
5. Then upload the `data` folder (with products.json inside)
6. Then upload the `assets` folder (with logo.svg inside)

For each upload:
- Click "Upload files"
- Drag the file/folder
- Click "Commit changes"

---

### **STEP 3: Go To Netlify** 

1. Open this link: https://app.netlify.com
2. Click "Log In"
3. Click "GitHub"
4. This connects your GitHub to Netlify

---

### **STEP 4: Deploy Your Website**

1. After logging in to Netlify
2. Click **"New site from Git"** (big button)
3. It says "Connect to Git provider" - choose **"GitHub"**
4. It will show your repositories
5. Find and click **"MAXVOLT"** repository
6. Click **"Deploy site"**
7. Wait 2-5 minutes...

---

### **STEP 5: Get Your Live Website!**

After deployment completes:

1. You'll see a URL like: `xxx-xxx-xxx.netlify.app`
2. **This is your live website!** 🎉
3. Share this URL with customers
