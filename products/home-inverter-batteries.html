Understanding Home Inverter Batteries

What is a Home Inverter Battery?

A home inverter battery stores electrical energy and provides backup power during outages. It works with an inverter (which converts DC to AC power) to keep your home running when the main electricity supply is interrupted.

Types of Batteries

  • Tubular Batteries: More durable, longer lifespan (4-5 years), better for frequent power cuts. Premium option.
  • Flat Plate Batteries: More affordable, shorter lifespan (2-3 years), suitable for occasional backups. Budget option.

Choosing the Right Capacity

Battery capacity in Ah (Amp-hours) determines how long your backup can last. Larger capacity = longer backup time.

  • 100-120Ah: Basic home backup, few appliances
  • 150Ah: Standard home, typical household load
  • 180-200Ah: Higher requirements, larger appliances, longer backup needed
