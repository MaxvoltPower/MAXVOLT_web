Understanding E-Bike Batteries

Voltage Configuration

E-bike batteries are sold as 12V units that are connected in series to achieve higher voltages:

  • 48V System: 4 × 12V batteries = 48V (most common for e-bikes)
  • 60V System: 5 × 12V batteries = 60V (higher power e-bikes)
  • 72V System: 6 × 12V batteries = 72V (high-performance e-bikes)

Capacity & Range

Battery capacity (Ah) determines how far you can ride:

  • 24Ah: 60-80 km range (casual users)
  • 28Ah: 80-100 km range (daily commuters)
  • 33Ah+: 100+ km range (frequent users, hilly terrain)
💡 Note: Actual range depends on terrain, rider weight, speed, and weather conditions. MAXVOLT can help you choose the right capacity for your typical usage.

Popular Brands

Care & Maintenance

  • Charge your battery after each use to maintain health
  • Avoid over-charging or completely draining the battery
  • Store in cool, dry place when not in use
  • Regular maintenance extends battery life by 20-30%
