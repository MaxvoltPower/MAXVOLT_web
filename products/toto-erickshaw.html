Understanding TOTO & E-Rickshaw Batteries

Voltage & Configuration

E-rickshaws use multiple 12V batteries connected in series to create higher voltages:

  • 36V System: 3 batteries × 12V = 36V (older/smaller rickshaws)
  • 48V System: 4 batteries × 12V = 48V (most common)
  • 60V System: 5 batteries × 12V = 60V (larger vehicles)
  • 72V System: 6 batteries × 12V = 72V (heavy-duty)
⚠️ Important: Do not mix different battery brands or ages in the same vehicle. All batteries must be identical for safe operation.

Battery Types

Tubular Lead-Acid

Traditional, reliable, cost-effective. Typical lifespan: 2-3 years with proper maintenance.

Lithium-Ion

Premium option, longer lifespan (5+ years), lighter weight, better range. Higher initial cost, excellent for frequent users.

Popular Brands

  • Exide E-Ride: Heavy-duty tubles, reliable for TOTO
  • Eastman: Designed for e-rickshaws, good performance
  • Luminous Cruze: Premium lithium option, excellent range

Maintenance Tips

  • Regular charging extends battery life
  • Do not over-discharge or over-charge
  • Check water level regularly (for lead-acid batteries)
  • Keep batteries clean and dry
  • Get professional check-ups quarterly
