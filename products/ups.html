Understanding UPS Systems

What is a UPS?

A UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) is a device that provides instant backup power when the main electricity supply fails. It gives you time to save your work and safely shut down your computer.

Key Specifications

  • VA Rating: Measured in Volt-Amperes (VA). Higher VA = more devices supported. 600-1100VA typical for home/office
  • Runtime: How long UPS can power your devices. Typically 10-20 minutes depending on load
  • Waveform: Simulated Sine or Pure Sine. Pure Sine is better for sensitive electronics
  • Battery Backup Time: Time between power failure and complete battery drain

Choosing the Right UPS

600-800VA

Desktop PC + Monitor + Router. Runtime: 10-15 minutes. Good for basic protection and safe shutdown.

1000-1100VA

Office workstation, multiple devices, or server. Runtime: 15-20 minutes. Better for extended work during outage.

1600VA+

Small server rooms, multiple workstations, or mission-critical systems. Professional-grade protection.
