Important Information About Car Batteries

Terminal Position (LEFT / RIGHT)

The terminal position (where the cables attach) is critical. It must match your car's battery connector location.

  • LEFT Terminal: Positive terminal on left side (common in Maruti, Hyundai, Renault)
  • RIGHT Terminal: Positive terminal on right side (common in Toyota, Mahindra)
⚠️ Important: Using the wrong terminal position can cause serious electrical issues. Always confirm the terminal position before purchase.

Standard Car Battery Specifications

Most cars use 12V batteries with capacity around 40-50Ah. The specific model depends on your vehicle requirements.
